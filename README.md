# Mansi Singh Thakur — Creative Portfolio

A production-ready editorial creative portfolio website designed and built for **Mansi Singh Thakur**, Computer Science student working across digital design, visual content, graphic design, café branding, website concepts, presentation design, image editing, and short-form video editing.

## ✦ Art Direction & Design System

- **Visual Direction:** Inspired by editorial publication layouts, warm paper textures, and distinctive café aesthetics.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Warm Cream: `#F6E8BD`
  - Butter Yellow: `#F4D978`
  - Muted Periwinkle: `#6879A5`
  - Dusty Blue: `#8E9DBB`
  - Deep Navy: `#202C49`
  - Warm Paper: `#F7F1E6`
  - Amber Accent: `#C58A20`
  - Café Espresso Brown: `#3C2618`
  - Muted Olive: `#59613B`
- **Typography:**
  - Display Headings: *Bebas Neue* & *Anton*
  - Body Text: *Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Inter*
  - Editorial Accents: *Instrument Serif* & *Playfair Display*
